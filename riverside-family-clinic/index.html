Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment for urgent care?

No. Urgent care is walk-in during clinic hours. For routine visits and physicals, booking ahead is recommended so you can see your preferred provider.

How do I book or cancel an appointment?

Call (509) 555-0142, use the "Book Appointment" button on this page, or ask the chat assistant in the corner. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.

Do you see new patients?

Yes, we're accepting new patients of all ages. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of current medications to your first visit.

Can I get my lab results online?

Yes. Results are posted to our patient portal, typically within 24–48 hours. Your provider will call you directly about any result needing follow-up.

What should I do for a medical emergency?

For life-threatening emergencies call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Our urgent care handles non-emergency issues only.

Ready to see a provider?

Book online in under a minute, call our front desk, or ask our chat assistant for help finding the right visit type.
