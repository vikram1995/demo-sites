One convenient downtown location with free patient parking, easy transit access, and a friendly front desk ready to help you plan your visit.

Plan your visit

Hours & location

We're located at 218 Cedar Street, Riverside, WA 98801, two blocks from the Cedar Street transit stop with free patient parking behind the building. The entrance is wheelchair accessible.

Monday – Friday 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM
Saturday 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Sunday Urgent care only, 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM
Holidays Closed (except urgent care)

Phone: (509) 555-0142 · Email: care@riversidefamilyclinic.example

Getting here

Directions & parking

  • Free patient parking behind the building, off Birch Alley
  • Two blocks from the Cedar Street transit stop (routes 4 & 12)
  • Wheelchair-accessible entrance and exam rooms

  • Drop-off lane at the Cedar Street entrance
