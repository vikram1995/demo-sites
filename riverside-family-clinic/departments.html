Primary Care

Family Medicine

Your medical home for every age. Family medicine providers handle annual physicals, ongoing management of chronic conditions, and coordinate any specialty referrals you need. Same-day sick visits are usually available.

  • Annual wellness & physical exams
  • Diabetes & prediabetes management
  • High blood pressure & cholesterol
  • Asthma & COPD care
  • Thyroid & hormone concerns
  • Preventive screenings & referrals

Children's Health

Pediatrics

From newborn checkups to the teenage years, our pediatric team partners with families on healthy growth and development. We follow the full CDC/AAP immunization schedule and offer school and sports physicals year-round.

  • Newborn & well-child visits
  • Growth & developmental screening
  • Childhood immunizations
  • School & sports physicals
  • Ear infections & common illness
  • ADHD & behavioral guidance

OB-GYN

Women's Health

Comprehensive care through every stage of life, delivered with a gentle, unhurried approach. Our OB-GYN team supports routine wellness, family planning, pregnancy, and menopause.

  • Well-woman & pelvic exams
  • Contraception counseling
  • Prenatal & postpartum care
  • Menopause management
  • Cervical & breast screenings
  • Bone density evaluation

Meet our OB-GYN team →

Walk-in Open now

Urgent Care

No appointment needed for non-emergency illness and injury. Our walk-in clinic treats most minor conditions with an average wait under 20 minutes, and can run rapid tests on site.

  • Colds, flu & sore throat
  • Minor cuts & sprains
  • Ear & sinus infections
  • Rapid strep & COVID-19 testing
  • Minor burns & rashes
  • Work & travel physicals

Heart & Vascular

Cardiology

Visiting cardiology clinic for heart-health evaluation and ongoing management. Our cardiologists treat common heart problems and help stabilize high cholesterol and high blood pressure, coordinating closely with your primary care provider.

  • Heart disease risk assessment
  • High blood pressure & angina
  • EKG & Holter monitoring
  • Echocardiogram (on-site)
  • Cholesterol management
  • Post-event follow-up care

Meet our cardiologist →

Mental & Behavioral

Behavioral Health

Confidential support for emotional wellbeing, integrated with your primary care so mind and body are treated together. We offer counseling, medication management, and referrals to specialty care.

  • Anxiety & depression
  • Stress & sleep concerns
  • Medication management
  • Individual counseling
  • Substance-use screening
  • Grief & life transitions

Meet our behavioral health team →

🦴
Bone, Joint & Sports

Orthopedics & Sports Medicine

Evaluation and non-surgical treatment of muscle, bone, and joint injuries, plus sports-medicine care to get active patients back in the game. On-site X-ray supports same-visit diagnosis.

  • Sprains, strains & fractures
  • Joint & back pain
  • Sports injury evaluation
  • Bracing & casting
  • Joint injections
  • Physical therapy referrals

Meet our orthopedics team →

🧴
Skin Health

Dermatology

Medical dermatology for skin, hair, and nail concerns, including annual skin-cancer screening. Suspicious spots can be biopsied during your visit.

  • Acne & eczema
  • Skin-cancer screening
  • Mole & lesion removal
  • Rashes & dermatitis
  • Psoriasis management
  • Skin biopsies

Meet our dermatologist →

🧪
Diagnostics

Laboratory Services

A full on-site lab means most blood work is drawn and processed in house, with results typically available in 24–48 hours through the patient portal.

  • Complete blood count (CBC)
  • Cholesterol / lipid panel
  • A1C & glucose testing
  • Thyroid panels
  • Pregnancy testing
  • Rapid strep & COVID-19

Lab services & cost →

Radiology

Imaging & Radiology

On-site digital X-ray and ultrasound support fast diagnosis without a separate trip. Images are read by our partnered radiology group, usually the same day.

  • Digital X-ray
  • Diagnostic ultrasound
  • Bone-density (DEXA) referral
  • Fracture & injury imaging
  • Chest & sinus imaging
  • Same-day radiology reads
